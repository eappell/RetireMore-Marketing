# AI Image Prompts for Medium Articles

Use these prompts in Midjourney, DALL-E, or Adobe Firefly. Add `photorealistic, 35mm lens, natural lighting, no text, no watermark` to any prompt for consistent quality.

**Aspect ratio guide:**
- **Hero images:** 16:9 (Medium's default featured image ratio, displays well on feeds and article headers)
- **In-article wide/landscape:** 3:2 (standard editorial photo, works well inline with text)
- **In-article square/intimate:** 4:5 (good for close-ups, still life, and portrait-oriented shots)

In Midjourney, append `--ar 16:9`, `--ar 3:2`, or `--ar 4:5`. In DALL-E, select the closest preset (wide, landscape, or portrait).

## General Tips

- Avoid stock photo cliches: no pointing at screens, no handshakes, no stethoscopes on money
- People should look like real Gen Xers, not models. Some gray hair, laugh lines, dressed casually
- Warm color palettes work best. Avoid cold blues and clinical whites.
- If generating couples, show genuine connection. Holding hands, leaning toward each other, laughing together.
- For solo shots, contemplative and purposeful beats posed and smiling.
