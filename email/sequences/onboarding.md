## Email 2 — Day 1 — The Most Common Mistake

**Subject:** The $100K Social Security mistake
**Preview text:** Most people leave money on the table without realizing it.
**Goal:** Click into the SS Optimizer or read the blog post

**Body:**

Yesterday I sent you a welcome email. Today, the single most common retirement mistake we see.

If your Full Retirement Age is 67 and your full Social Security benefit is $2,500/mo, here's what claiming at different ages does:

- Claim at 62 → $1,750/mo (30% reduction, permanent)
- Claim at 67 → $2,500/mo (your full benefit)
- Claim at 70 → $3,100/mo (24% increase, permanent)

That's a $1,350/mo difference between claiming at 62 vs 70. Over a 20-year retirement, that's $324,000.

The break-even is around age 80–82. If you live past that, every month after is upside.

Most people never run their own numbers. If you haven't, our Social Security Optimizer takes about 5 minutes.
