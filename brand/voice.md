# RetireMore Voice Guide

The voice is **forward-looking, holistic, specific, and quietly confident.** Retirement is the next chapter — not an ending, not a crisis. We are the calm, well-researched friend who has actually run the numbers.

## The 5 voice attributes

1. **Forward-looking** — Frame retirement as opportunity, not loss. "What's possible" before "what's at risk."
2. **Holistic** — Money is one of five dimensions: income, healthcare, location, purpose, legacy. We talk about all of them. Always.
3. **Specific** — Real numbers, real ages, real scenarios. Vague claims are forbidden.
4. **Quietly confident** — No hype, no exclamation points, no "shocking truth," no urgency manipulation.
5. **Plainspoken** — Sixth-grade reading level when possible. Define jargon the first time it appears.

## Do

- **Use real numbers.** "Claiming Social Security at 62 vs 70 changes lifetime benefits by ~$324K over 20 years (assuming a $2,500 FRA benefit)" beats "Social Security claiming has a big impact."
- **Cite primary sources.** SSA, CMS, BLS, NBER, Boston College Center for Retirement Research, Kitces, Morningstar, Vanguard research, peer-reviewed journals.
- **Name the trade-off.** Every recommendation has a downside. Acknowledge it.
- **Use the second person.** "You" not "people" or "retirees."
- **Link to the relevant tool** at least once per article. Soft CTA, not a hard sell.
- **Define acronyms on first use:** RMD (required minimum distribution), IRMAA (income-related Medicare premium), FRA (full retirement age), Roth conversion, etc.
- **Use contractions.** "You're" not "you are." It reads like a person.
- **End with what to actually do.** Every piece should leave the reader with a specific next step.

## Don't

- **No exclamation points.** Anywhere. Even in CTAs.
- **No fear-mongering.** Don't lead with "you'll outlive your money." Lead with "here's what sustainable looks like."
- **No urgency manipulation.** No "limited time," no "act now," no fake scarcity, no countdown timers.
- **No "shocking" / "secret" / "they don't want you to know."** We are not a clickbait operation.
- **No specific securities recommendations.** Ever. Don't name funds, tickers, or "buy this" advice.
- **No promises of returns.** Never "guaranteed," never "risk-free," never "8% per year."
- **No claiming endorsement** by SSA, CMS, IRS, AARP, or any government body.
- **No "Boomers vs. Gen X" framing.** Our audience spans both. Don't divide.
- **No anti-advisor framing.** Some readers have great advisors. We're additive, not adversarial. ("Even if you have an advisor, it helps to bring numbers to the conversation.")
- **No emojis in long-form copy.** They're allowed in social posts and email subject lines if used sparingly (≤1 per piece).

## Compliance rails (always true)

Add this disclaimer to any piece that gives specific numerical guidance:

> *This is a planning tool, not personalized financial advice. For decisions involving your specific situation, consider consulting a fiduciary financial planner.*

Other rails:
- We don't sell insurance, annuities, securities, or managed accounts.
- We don't take referral fees from financial product issuers.
- We don't share user data with advertisers.
- We are not a registered investment advisor (RIA).

## Tone by channel

| Channel | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Long-form blog | Friendly expert. Slightly more formal. Cite sources. ~1,200–2,000 words. |
| Email (lifecycle) | Warmer, first-name. Shorter paragraphs. One CTA per email. |
| Email (cold outreach) | Direct, respectful, brief. Lead with relevance, not flattery. |
| Google Ads | Functional. Headlines name the outcome ("Compare 100+ countries to retire abroad"). No exclamation points. |
| Meta Ads | Slightly warmer, image-led. Lead with the holistic angle, not just money. |
| LinkedIn post | Professional, insight-led, specific. Personal stories OK. |
| X (Twitter) | Specific, punchy, one number per post. Threads for stats. |
| Reddit | Subreddit-aware. No promotion in r/financialindependence, r/retirement, r/Bogleheads — only contribute value. |
| YouTube script | Conversational. Hook in first 5 seconds with a specific number. |
| TikTok | Fast, specific, surprising. One claim per video. |

## Word lists

**Use:**
plan, model, project, compare, explore, decide, sustainable, holistic, specific, your numbers, the math, the trade-off, what's possible, the next chapter

**Avoid:**
crush it, hack, secret, shocking, must-read, ultimate guide, no-brainer, pro tip, game-changer, revolutionary, disrupt, unlock, supercharge, leverage (as a verb), guru

## Calibration examples

**Bad:** "Don't make this SHOCKING Social Security mistake that's costing retirees thousands! 🚨"

**Good:** "If your full retirement age is 67, claiming at 62 permanently reduces your benefit by 30%. Over a 20-year retirement, that's roughly $324,000 you don't get back."

---

**Bad:** "RetireMore is the ultimate AI-powered retirement platform that revolutionizes how you plan!"

**Good:** "RetireMore plans your whole retirement — income, taxes, healthcare, location, and legacy — in one place. Free to start. No advisor required."

---

**Bad:** "You only have 30 days to claim your retirement readiness score!"

**Good:** "Your readiness score updates as you complete each tool. Most people finish in about an hour."
