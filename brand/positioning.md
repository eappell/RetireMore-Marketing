# Positioning

## One-line positioning

> The only retirement platform that plans your **whole** retirement — finances, location, healthcare, purpose, and legacy — in one place.

## The wedge

Every other retirement tool treats retirement as a math problem. RetireMore treats it as a *life problem* that has math in it. The wedge is **holistic**: we're the only platform with serious tools for **identity, location, and purpose** sitting alongside the income, tax, and estate calculators.

## Frame

| Old way | RetireMore way |
|---|---|
| A spreadsheet for your portfolio | A whole-life plan with the spreadsheet built in |
| One calculator at a time, on different sites | 12 tools that talk to each other in one place |
| "How much do I need?" | "What kind of retirement do I want — and what does that take?" |
| An AUM advisor's intake form | A self-service plan you actually own |
| A free trial that locks features | A free tier that's actually useful |
| Generic content articles | The article *and* the tool to run your own numbers |

## Trust pillars (always cite)

1. **No financial products sold.** No insurance, no annuities, no managed accounts. Ever.
2. **No advisor required.** You can do the whole plan yourself. Our outputs work great in advisor meetings if you have one.
3. **Free to start.** All 12 tools on the free tier. The free tier is permanent, not a trial.

## Why now

- ~10,000 Americans turn 65 every day for the rest of this decade.
- Pension coverage has collapsed; most people are responsible for their own retirement plan with no real training.
- Financial advice is expensive (1% AUM = $10K/year on a $1M portfolio) and most advisors don't touch the non-financial side at all.
- AI finally makes it possible to give personalized planning to people who can't afford a $5K/year fee-only planner.

| **Boldin (NewRetirement)** | Comprehensive financial plan | Holistic — we cover identity, location, purpose. Free tier is more useful. |

| **Fee-only RIA** | Personalized human advice | $0 instead of $3K–$10K/year. Bring our outputs to them. |
| **AARP / NerdWallet content** | Massive reach, trusted brand | Articles + actual tools to run your numbers. |
| **A robo-advisor** | Automated portfolio management | We're a *planner*, not a *manager*. Different problem. |

## What we are NOT

- Not a robo-advisor
- Not an insurance brokerage
- Not a registered investment advisor
- Not a brokerage
- Not an ad-supported content site
- Not a "freemium SaaS" that gates the useful stuff

## Tagline

**Master tagline:** *More Income. More Purpose. More Clarity.*

**Alt taglines** (use sparingly, situationally):
- "Plan the whole retirement — not just the money."
- "Your retirement, in one place."
- "The retirement plan you actually own."

## Brand metaphors that work

- A *plan*, not a calculator
- A *map*, not a destination
- A *coach*, not a salesperson
- A *workshop*, not a sales pitch
- A *next chapter*, not an ending

## Brand metaphors that don't work (avoid)

- "Toolkit" (too generic)
- "Suite" (too corporate)
- "Solution" (too SaaS-y)
- Mountain-climbing / summit imagery (cliché)
- Sunset / golden years (saccharine)
- Anything involving golf or beaches (stereotype)
