## Value props by tool category

### Financial Security
- *Hook:* "Run the math on your real situation — before you make a decision you can't undo."
- *Proof:* Monte Carlo, SSA-grade benefit calc, IRMAA-aware tax modeling, sequence-of-returns testing.
- *Differentiator:* These tools talk to each other. A Roth conversion in the Tax Analyzer flows to your Healthcare and Income projections automatically (Planner tier).

### Lifestyle & Purpose
- *Hook:* "Retirement is more than a withdrawal rate. Where will you live? Who will you be? What will fill your days?"
- *Proof:* 100+ country dataset for Retire Abroad. State-by-state tax + cost data. Identity Builder grounded in modern psychology research (Brooks, Schlossberg, Modern Elder Academy framing).
- *Differentiator:* No other retirement tool takes the lifestyle and identity questions seriously. Most are calculators that pretend the rest doesn't exist.

### Legacy & Impact
- *Hook:* "Make it easy on the people you love."
- *Proof:* Sankey-style estate flow visualization, gifting math against current annual exclusion, digital estate vault with executor instructions.
- *Differentiator:* Legacy planning shouldn't require a $400 estate attorney consult to get started. Start with the picture, then go to the lawyer with the right questions.
