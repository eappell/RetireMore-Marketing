# Launch Creator Outreach — First 20

**Updated:** 2026-05-06
**Goal:** Recruit 10+ of these 20 creators into the affiliate program in the first 90 days post-launch.

## How to use this doc

1. Use the master template below as the starting email.
2. For Tier 1 creators (1 to 7), customize using the personalization grid. Consider running `/affiliate-pitch <channel-name>` to generate a bespoke pitch and creative kit.
3. Track outreach state in a simple Notion table or Google Sheet (template at bottom).
4. Follow up exactly twice on the timing schedule. No badgering.

## Master template (Touch 1: Cold email)

**From:** Eddie Appell, founder, RetireMore
**Reply-to:** webdev@appell.com (or partners@retiremore.com when set up)

**Subject options:**
- "30% recurring lifetime, no asset gate — would this work for [show name]?"
- "Affiliate proposal for [show name]: rate is 30% recurring lifetime"
- "[First name], are you taking on new affiliate partnerships?"

**Body (≤150 words):**

```
Hi [First name],

I've been listening to / reading [specific show or post + 1 sentence on what was useful]. Your audience is exactly who we built RetireMore for: people 50 to 70 figuring out the next chapter, not customers for AUM or insurance.

We launched our affiliate program at 30% recurring for the lifetime of every customer you refer, plus a $20 bonus on your first conversion. No 12-month cap, no $100k-asset gate, no "apply to learn the rate." For comparison, ProjectionLab pays 20% (likely capped), and Boldin doesn't publish theirs but their own marquee affiliate has called the rate too low to bother with.

If you're open, I'll send you a free Planner account for review and a creator kit. If not, no worries.

Either way, full rate card is at retiremore.com/affiliates.

Eddie Appell
Founder, RetireMore
```

```
Hi [First name],

Quick nudge in case my last note got buried. The short version: 30% recurring lifetime, $20 first-conversion bonus, public rate card at retiremore.com/affiliates. Happy to send a free Planner account so you can poke around.
```

For Tier 1 creators only, offer a custom code or co-marketing slot. For Tier 2+, send a final "no worries, here if you reconsider" close. Never invent artificial scarcity.

## Compliance reminders

- **Do not pitch fee-only CFPs or RIAs with the paid affiliate offer.** The fiduciary-conflict carve-out (per `AFFILIATE_PROGRAM.md` appendix and SEC Rule 206(4)-3) means CFPs use a referral or educational license, not paid affiliate.
- **All recipients above are content creators (publishers).** None of them are registered investment advisers based on public profiles. If any have RIA registrations, route to the educational-license path instead.
- **FTC disclosure** is required in every post they link from. Provide the canned language in Touch 1's creator kit.
- **No paid search bidding** on RetireMore brand terms. State this in the welcome email after they apply.
