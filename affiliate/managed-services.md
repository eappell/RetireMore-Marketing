# Managed Affiliate Services vs. DIY

**Updated:** 2026-05-06
**Question:** Should RetireMore run the affiliate program in-house or outsource to an agency?
**Recommendation:** **DIY through end of Year 1.** Reevaluate when monthly affiliate-driven revenue exceeds $50k or active partner count exceeds 100.

## Three tiers of "managed"

### Tier 1 — Enterprise agencies

**Examples:** Acceleration Partners, JEBCommerce, PartnerCentric, Versa Marketing.

**What they do:** End-to-end. Recruit, onboard, manage, optimize, fraud-detect, brand-safety, performance reporting. 2 to 5 dedicated people on the account. Multi-network expertise (Impact, CJ, ShareASale, Awin).

**Cost:** $5,000 to $10,000/mo retainer, sometimes plus 5 to 15% rev share. Typical contract: 12 months minimum. Setup fees often $5,000 to $25,000.

**Effectiveness:** Strong for scaled brands. Acceleration Partners has 30+ industry awards and manages programs for Target, Reebok, ButcherBox, Noom. Reviewers consistently praise their reporting rigor. Common complaint: junior account staff slow down communication.

**When it makes sense:**
- $200k+/mo in affiliate revenue
- Hundreds-to-thousands of active partners
- Multi-country expansion
- Fraud is a real workload at your scale

**When it doesn't:** RetireMore at launch. We don't have the volume to amortize the retainer. Brand-voice and compliance carve-outs (no commissions to fee-only fiduciaries, no advisor lead-gen) require in-house judgment.

### Tier 2 — Boutique OAMs (Outsourced Affiliate Managers)

**Examples:** OPM Team and similar specialty shops; freelance affiliate managers via Upwork or Mayple.

**What they do:** Same scope as Tier 1 but smaller team (1 to 2 people). Often use the brand's chosen platform rather than imposing their own.

**Cost:** $1,500 to $3,500/mo retainer, often plus 5 to 10% rev share. Some run pure rev-share at 10 to 20%.

**Effectiveness:** Good when the boutique has SaaS-subscription experience specifically. Generic e-commerce-trained OAMs often don't understand recurring-commission math or churn dynamics, which leads to bad partner-tier decisions.

**When it makes sense:**
- $20k to $200k/mo in affiliate revenue
- 50 to 200 active partners
- You want a pro running it but can't justify enterprise pricing

**When it doesn't:** Pre-launch or sub-$20k/mo programs. The retainer eats your margin before the program is paying back. Also: if your compliance is unusual (ours is), the OAM needs hand-holding for the first 60 to 90 days, which kills the time-savings rationale.

### Tier 3 — Platform-managed (light touch)

**Examples:** Refersion managed services, Impact agency add-on, PartnerStack assisted.

**What they do:** Platform you'd use anyway, plus a part-time human who helps with recruitment outreach, asset creation, and reporting interpretation. Sometimes called "PMS" (Partner Managed Services).

**Cost:** Platform base ($150 to $500/mo) plus managed services add-on ($1,500 to $3,500/mo).

**Effectiveness:** Lighter than Tier 2, more structured than DIY. Best as a bridge when you have the platform investment but not yet the headcount.

**When it makes sense:** You want help but want to keep core relationships in-house. Bridge between DIY and full agency.

## DIY costs (RetireMore's likely path)

| Component | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rewardful platform | $49 to $149/mo | Stripe-native, scales to mid-volume |
| 0.25 FTE marketing time | ~$2,000/mo loaded | Recruitment outreach, asset updates, monthly affiliate scorecard |
| Asset production | ~$500/mo | Banners, video clips, copy refreshes |
| Payouts | Pass-through | Wise/PayPal fees ~1 to 2% of payout volume |
| **Total** | **~$2,500 to $3,000/mo all-in** | At launch through ~50 active affiliates |

That same $2,500 to $3,000 is the floor for a Tier 2 boutique OAM monthly retainer (and that doesn't include the platform). DIY is materially cheaper at our stage.

## Effectiveness comparison (honest read)

Managed services are mostly about **scale and operational hygiene**, not creative wins. An agency will not find creators we can't find. They will:

- Run the program more consistently when we're busy with other things.
- Catch fraud sooner (account-stuffing, refund clusters).
- Handle multi-network attribution if we expand to Impact / CJ.
- Negotiate better terms with influencer agencies.

For a brand like RetireMore where:
- The category is sleepy (we don't need agency relationships to find partners),
- The audience is narrow (50 to 70, retirement-curious),
- Compliance carve-outs require nuance (fee-only fiduciary policy, no advisor matching),

…the value of an agency in year 1 is materially lower than for a generic e-commerce brand. The relationships that drive this program (Rob Berger, WCI, Holy Schmidt, etc.) are won by founder direct outreach, not by agency cold-emailing.

## Recommendation

**Year 1: DIY with Rewardful + 0.25 FTE.** Founder runs Tier 1 creator outreach personally. Marketing operations runs Tier 2+ outreach via templates. Loops handles affiliate scorecard email.

**Year 2 reevaluation triggers (any one):**
1. Monthly affiliate-driven revenue exceeds $50k.
2. Active partners exceed 100 and recruitment is the bottleneck.
3. Fraud detection becomes a real workload (suspicious sign-up patterns, refund clusters).
4. International expansion (European, Canadian, Australian creators).

**At reevaluation, prefer Tier 2 boutique OAM over Tier 1 enterprise.** The agency that wins for us is one that respects our brand voice and compliance rails, not one optimized for Fortune 500 retainer-fee preservation. Specifically: pre-screen for SaaS-subscription experience and ability to articulate why fee-only fiduciary kickbacks are forbidden.
