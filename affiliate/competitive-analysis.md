**Updated:** 2026-05-06
**Purpose:** Set RetireMore's affiliate rates against verified public competitor data.

## Read on the category

1. **Sleepy.** Half the obvious comps either hide their rate or have no program at all. There's no aggressive challenger.
2. **ProjectionLab is the rate-setter.** 20% recurring on FirstPromoter. Creators benchmark RetireMore against them.
3. **Empower's $250 looks huge but is mostly inaccessible.** The $100k-asset gate kills conversion for most retirement-content audiences (people watching "how to live on $4k/mo in retirement" are not the asset-rich crowd).
4. **Boldin's main weakness is opacity plus low absolute dollars.** WCI publicly griping is a marketing gift we can use directly in outreach.

## Implications for RetireMore's rate card

To beat ProjectionLab credibly while staying within sustainable margin:
- **30% recurring lifetime** (vs ProjectionLab's 20%, possibly capped).
- **$20 first-conversion bonus** to fix the "first paycheck takes 3 months and is $4" micro-influencer problem.
- **Tier escalators 30/35/40%** at 0/25/100 active subs. Rewards growth without burying small partners (which is Boldin's failure mode).
- **90-day cookie** vs Empower's 30 and most retail's 30 to 60. Retirement decision cycle is long.
- **Public rate card** at retiremore.com/affiliates. Boldin's main weakness flipped into our recruiting wedge.
