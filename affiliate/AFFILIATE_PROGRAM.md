# RetireMore — Affiliate & Referral Program

**Prepared:** March 2026
**Status:** Planning — launch alongside or shortly after public launch

---

## Program Overview

RetireMore's affiliate program pays partners for driving qualified signups. The program is structured in two tiers:

1. **Consumer affiliates** — paid per user who signs up for a paid plan (Standard or Premium)
2. **Professional affiliates** — paid per advisor who subscribes to an Advisor Seat or Growth Plan

All affiliates receive a unique referral link, a real-time dashboard showing clicks/conversions/earnings, and marketing assets (banners, copy, video clips).

## Commission Structure

### Consumer Referrals

| Event | Commission | Cookie Window | Notes |
|-------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Free signup | $0 | — | No commission on free tier (too easy to game) |
| Standard plan ($19/mo or $149/yr) | **$20 per conversion** | 60 days | Paid after user's first payment clears |
| Premium plan ($29/mo or $229/yr) | **$35 per conversion** | 60 days | Paid after user's first payment clears |
| Recurring bonus (optional, Year 2+) | **10% of subscription** for 12 months | — | Incentivizes affiliates to promote retention |

### Professional Referrals (Advisor-to-Advisor)

| Event | Commission | Cookie Window | Notes |
|-------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Advisor Seat ($199/mo) | **$200 per conversion** | 90 days | Paid after advisor's first month payment |
| Growth Plan ($349/mo) | **$350 per conversion** | 90 days | Paid after advisor's first month payment |
| Recurring bonus | **10% of subscription** for 12 months | — | Strong incentive for advisor-to-advisor word-of-mouth |

### Payment Terms

- Minimum payout threshold: $50
- Payment methods: PayPal, bank transfer (ACH), or Stripe Connect
- Payment frequency: Monthly (net 30 after conversion is confirmed)
- Chargeback/refund clawback: if the referred user cancels within 30 days, commission is reversed

## Affiliate Channels

### 1. Retirement Content Creators (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram)

**Who:** Creators making content about retirement planning, financial independence, FIRE movement, or "life after 50" content.

**Why they'd promote RetireMore:** Their audience is actively planning retirement and asking the exact questions RetireMore answers. Promoting a comprehensive tool (not just a calculator) differentiates their content.

**Commission:** $20–$35 per paid signup

**Outreach strategy:**
- Identify creators with 10K–500K followers in the retirement/financial planning niche
- Offer free Premium access for review
- Provide custom landing pages and promo codes (e.g., `retiremore.com/creator-name`)
- Supply short demo videos and talking points they can adapt
- Offer exclusive "first 100 users" discount codes for their audience

**Target creators (examples):**
- YouTube: retirement lifestyle channels, "how to retire early" creators, financial planning educators
- TikTok: personal finance creators targeting 40–60 age range (growing demographic on TikTok)
- Instagram: retirement coaches, lifestyle-over-50 accounts, financial literacy educators

**KPIs:** 50–100 creator partnerships in Year 1, targeting 5–10 conversions per creator per month

### 5. CFP & Financial Advisor Referrals (Advisor-to-Advisor)

**Who:** CFPs, RIAs, and financial planners who use RetireMore professionally and refer other advisors.

**Why they'd refer:** Strong financial incentive ($200–$350 per advisor signup + 10% recurring for 12 months). Advisors talk to other advisors — conferences, study groups, LinkedIn, industry forums.

**Commission:** $200–$350 per advisor signup + 10% recurring for 12 months

**Program structure:**
- Existing advisor customers automatically get a referral link
- First 3 months free for the referred advisor (reduces friction)
- Referring advisor earns commission after the referred advisor's first paid month
- Tiered bonus: refer 5+ advisors in a quarter → additional $500 bonus
- Annual "Top Referrer" recognition (featured in newsletter, conference invite)

**Outreach strategy:**
- Include referral CTA in advisor onboarding flow
- Referral card in advisor dashboard ("Invite a colleague — earn $200")
- Conference networking: provide referral cards at FPA, NAPFA events
- LinkedIn outreach: "Ask your advisor friends if they've seen this"

**KPIs:** 30% of advisor customers participate in referral program, 2–3 referrals per active referrer per year

## Compliance & Legal

- **FTC disclosure:** All affiliates must disclose their relationship with RetireMore in content (required by FTC)
- **Affiliate agreement:** Standard terms covering: prohibited tactics (spam, misleading claims), brand usage, payment terms, termination
- **Financial disclaimers:** Affiliates may NOT present RetireMore as financial advice — they must use approved language: "RetireMore is an educational planning tool, not a substitute for professional financial advice"
- **No incentivized signups:** Affiliates may not offer cash/gift incentives for signups beyond the discount codes we provide
- **Geographic restrictions:** US-only program at launch (international affiliates in Year 2)
