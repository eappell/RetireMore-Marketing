# Social Content Calendar

The rolling weekly grid. Append new weeks at the bottom; never delete history. Each cell points to the post slug in `social/<channel>/`.

## Posting cadence (target)

| Channel | Cadence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| X (Twitter) | 5 posts + 1 thread/week | Algorithm rewards consistency |
| LinkedIn | 3 posts/week, 1 article/month | Professional audience, slower cadence |
| YouTube | 1 long video/week + 2 shorts | Hard to do well; quality over volume |
| TikTok | 3–5 shorts/week | Algorithm rewards volume |
| Reddit | 2 value contributions/week | Never link-drop; only valuable answers |
