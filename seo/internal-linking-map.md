## Hub-and-spoke pattern

Each pillar post is the **hub**. Supporting posts link UP to the hub. Hub links DOWN to all supporting posts. Every post links sideways to the relevant *tool landing page* (`retiremore.com/tools/<slug>`) and to the *demo* (`/demo`).

## Anchor text rules

- Use descriptive anchor text, not "click here."
- Mix exact-match and partial-match for the primary keyword (over-optimized exact-match looks spammy).
- Tool links: use the tool's product name. Example: "[Social Security Optimizer](https://retiremore.com/tools/social-security-optimizer)"
- Blog cross-links: use the post title or a paraphrase.
